# 🚀 AI Engineering Journey

> **Documenting my journey from a Computer Science student to a Production-Ready AI Engineer.**

Welcome! 👋

This repository is a public record of my journey into **Artificial Intelligence**, **Machine Learning**, **Deep Learning**, **Large Language Models (LLMs)**, and **AI Engineering**.

My goal is not just to complete courses, but to understand how AI systems work by learning the fundamentals, implementing important algorithms from scratch, building real-world projects, and documenting everything I learn along the way.

---

## 🎯 Mission

To become a **Production-Ready AI Engineer** capable of designing, building, deploying, and maintaining intelligent systems that solve real-world problems.

I believe in **learning by building**, where every concept is reinforced through code, notes, experiments, and practical projects.

## 📌 Learning Principles

* Learn from first principles.
* Implement important concepts from scratch.
* Use industry-standard libraries for practical development.
* Build after every major concept.
* Write clean, readable, and well-documented code.
* Commit progress consistently.
* Focus on understanding rather than memorization.
* Test implementations instead of assuming they work.
* Learn through experiments and failure.
* Prioritize long-term consistency over short-term speed.
* Build production-oriented systems, not just tutorial projects.
